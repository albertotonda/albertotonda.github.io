The [Evolutionary Computation in Practice](https://sequential-parameter-optimization.github.io/evolutionary-computation-in-practice/) (ECiP) workshop is a long-standing presence at the [Genetic and Evolutionary Computation COnference (GECCO)](https://gecco-2026.sigevo.org/HomePage), aiming at showcasing how evolutionary algorithms (EAs) are used in industrial practice. This year, the conference was in Costa Rica, and I had the pleasure of being invited as a speaker by the organizers, [Thomas Bartz-Beielstein](https://www.th-koeln.de/en/person/thomas.bartz-beielstein/), [Daniel E. Hernández](https://sites.google.com/tectijuana.edu.mx/danielehernandez), and [Francisco Fernández de Vega](https://grupogea.unex.es/index.php/componentes/francisco-fernandez-de-vega/). I presented several works I was involved in, where EAs have been successfully employed in [robotics]({{< relref "/publication/giovannitti-2022-virtual/index.md" >}}), [industrial processing]({{< relref "/publication/jarmatz-2024-development/index.md" >}}), and [biology]({{< relref "project/2025-MuSiHC">}}).
