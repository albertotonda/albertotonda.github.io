I did not submit a single paper to [NeurIPS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_on_Neural_Information_Processing_Systems) this year, and honestly I had no plans to attend. Then I (susprisingly) received an email informing me that, based on my performance as a reviewer, I have been granted a free registration!

This is a nice initiative, which both encourages and recognizes reviewers' efforts, and I hope that more conferences will follow NeurIPS' example. I will then be attending [NeurIPS 2026](https://neurips.cc/) online.
