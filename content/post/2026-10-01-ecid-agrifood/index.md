I participated in an event organized at the Ministry of Agriculture in Paris, opened by the French Minister of Agriculture, [Annie Genevard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annie_Genevard).

![The French Minister for Agriculture, Annie Genevard](annie-genevard.jpg)

The event presented the start of the [EDIC](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/edic) AgriFood, a European legal instrument aiming at harmonizing agri-food data across all participant countries, in line with the broader European effort to build a [Common European Agricultural Data Space](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/policy-brief-rolling-out-common-european-agricultural-data-space).

The press release for the event, with more information (in French), can be found [here](https://agriculture.gouv.fr/lancement-de-ledic-agri-food).
